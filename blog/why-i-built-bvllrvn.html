Why I Built BVLLRVN

Oct 9, 2026 · Matthew

I build BVLLRVN in the hours nobody sees: early mornings, late nights, the margins of a full life.

To me, the name means grit and betting on yourself. A bull run is a bet that things go up. BVLLRVN is that bet, placed on you.

This season of my life is a rebirth. I'm rising toward dreams I used to set aside, and I can feel the world about to change. I don't want to watch that change from the sidelines. I want to seize it while others sleep, and build while others fight to hold on to comfort and mediocrity.

That's what this brand is for. It's not about the noise or the applause. It's for the person deciding to become someone new and doing the quiet work to get there.

This is our time to build a new world, and a new version of ourselves to rise into.
Est. in the climb, not the peak. — Matthew
